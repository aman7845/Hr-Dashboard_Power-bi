<h2><a class="anchor" id="overview"></a>Overview</h2>
The **HR Analytics Dashboard** provides a comprehensive view of workforce data to identify patterns and factors contributing to employee attrition.  
Developed using **Power BI** and **Excel**, this project helps Human Resource teams make informed, data-driven decisions to improve retention, satisfaction, and workforce stability.

---

<h2><a class="anchor" id="objective"></a>Objective</h2>
The main objective of this project is to analyze employee demographics, job roles, salary bands, and satisfaction levels to determine the key drivers of attrition within the company.  
The dashboard offers a visual summary of trends and helps management create targeted strategies for employee engagement and retention.

---

<h2><a class="anchor" id="dashboard-analysis-questions"></a>Dashboard Analysis Question</h2>

<details>
<summary> Click to view all 20 analytical questions</summary>

### General Overview
1. What is the total number of employees and the overall attrition rate?  
2. Which department has the highest attrition — Human Resources, Research & Development, or Sales?  
3. What is the average age, salary, and tenure of employees?  

### Attrition by Education
4. Which educational background has the highest attrition rate?  
5. Which education category shows the lowest attrition and why?  

### Attrition by Age
6. Which age group experiences the highest attrition?  
7. What could be the possible reasons for higher attrition in the 26–35 age group?  

### Attrition by Salary
8. How does attrition vary with different salary ranges?  
9. Does higher salary correspond to lower attrition levels?  

### Attrition by Job Role
10. Which job roles are most affected by attrition?  
11. How does attrition compare between Laboratory Technicians and Sales Executives?  

### Job Satisfaction
12. Which job role has the highest number of dissatisfied employees?  
13. How does job satisfaction correlate with attrition across departments?  

### Attrition by Time Duration
14. During which years of service is attrition highest?  
15. Are employees more likely to leave early in their tenure?  

### Attrition by Gender
16. Which gender has a higher attrition rate, and why might that be?  
17. Is attrition by gender consistent across all departments?  

### Analytical / Interpretation
18. What are the top three factors contributing to attrition?  
19. How can HR use these insights to improve retention?  
20. What recommendations can be made to reduce attrition in the most affected departments?  

</details>

<h2><a class="anchor" id="insights-summary"></a>Insight Summary</h2>
- The highest attrition is observed among employees aged **26–35 years**, particularly in **technical and sales roles**.  
- Employees with **lower salary ranges** and **shorter tenure** show a greater tendency to leave.  
- **Job satisfaction** and **career growth opportunities** emerge as critical factors in retention.  
- Targeted HR strategies such as **improving pay structure, training, and engagement programs** can reduce turnover significantly.
